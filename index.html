关于 App About the App

花朵识别帮助你通过照片识别花朵，并记录你发现的花朵。 你可以拍摄照片或从照片中选择图片，查看识别结果，并将成功识别的花朵收藏到自己的花朵图鉴中。

Flower Recognition helps you identify flowers from photos and keep track of the flowers you discover. Take a photo or choose an image, view the recognition result, and collect successful discoveries in your personal flower collection.

常见问题 Common Questions

  • 为什么 App 无法识别我的花朵？
    当照片无法提供足够的信息进行可靠识别时，App 可能不会返回识别结果。 建议使用清晰、光线充足且花朵主体完整的照片。
  • Why didn't the app identify my flower?
    Recognition may be unavailable when an image does not provide enough information for a reliable identification. Try a clear, well-lit photo with the flower visible and in focus.
  • 为什么识别结果可能不准确？
    图像识别并非始终完美。如果结果不符合预期，可以尝试从不同角度拍摄一张更清晰的照片。
  • Why is the recognition result inaccurate?
    Image-based recognition is not perfect. Try another photo showing the flower from a different angle or with fewer distracting objects.
  • 为什么 App 无法访问相机或照片？
    请前往 iPhone 的“设置”，检查是否已经允许 App 使用相机或访问照片。
  • Why can't the app access my camera or photos?
    Open your iPhone Settings and make sure the app has permission to access the Camera or Photos.
  • 如何保存花朵到我的图鉴？
    成功识别后，对应的花朵卡片会被解锁并加入你的收藏。
  • How do I save a flower to my collection?
    A successful recognition can unlock a flower card in your collection.
