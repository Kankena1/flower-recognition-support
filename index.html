About the App

Flower Recognition helps you identify flowers from photos and keep track of the flowers you discover. Take a photo or choose an image, view the recognition result, and collect successful discoveries in your flower collection.

Common Questions

  • Why didn't the app identify my flower?
    Recognition may be unavailable when an image does not provide enough information for a reliable identification. Try a clear, well-lit photo with the flower visible and in focus.
  • Why is the recognition result inaccurate?
    Image-based recognition is not perfect. Try another photo showing the flower from a different angle or with fewer distractions in the background.
  • Why can't the app access my camera or photos?
    Check your iPhone Settings and make sure the app has permission to access the Camera or Photos.
  • How do I save a flower to my collection?
    A successful recognition can unlock a flower card in the app's collection.
