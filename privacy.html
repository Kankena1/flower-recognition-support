Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Luvplants ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App.

We respect your privacy and have designed Luvplants so that plant image recognition is performed locally on your device.

Luvplants does not require an account and, in the current version of the App, plant photographs are not uploaded to our servers for plant identification.

2. Camera Access

Luvplants may request permission to access your device's camera.

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photograph of a plant for identification.

You may deny or revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings.

3. Photo Library Access

Luvplants allows you to select an existing photograph from your device's photo library for plant identification.

The App only processes photographs that you actively choose through the image selection interface.

4. Plant Image Recognition

Plant images captured or selected through Luvplants are processed using an on-device machine learning model.

Plant identification is performed locally on your device. The current version of the App does not send plant photographs to our servers for image recognition.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

When a plant is successfully identified, Luvplants may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Plant photographs;
  • Thumbnail versions of plant photographs;
  • The identified plant name;
  • The identification confidence score;
  • The date of identification;
  • The number of times a plant has been discovered.

This information is used to provide features such as your plant collection, identification history, and plant discovery cards.

6. Data Transmission and Sharing

In the current version of Luvplants, we do not operate a server that receives your plant photographs or locally stored plant identification history.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your plant photographs or identification history for advertising or user tracking.

We do not intentionally share your locally stored plant records with advertising networks, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

7. Accounts and Personal Information

The current version of Luvplants does not require users to create an account.

The App does not intentionally request personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or physical address in order to use its core plant identification features.

8. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current version of Luvplants does not intentionally include third-party advertising systems or behavioral advertising features.

Luvplants does not use your data for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Plant photographs and identification records are stored locally on your device.

Luvplants provides features that allow you to delete plant records and associated images.

Deleting the App may also remove locally stored App data, subject to Apple's operating system behavior and any backup settings controlled by the user.

Because the current version of Luvplants does not maintain user accounts or store these records on our own servers, we generally do not possess a remote copy of your local plant collection.

10. Device Permissions

You can manage permissions granted to Luvplants through the Settings app on your device.

If camera access is disabled, features that require taking new plant photographs may not function.

11. Children's Privacy

Luvplants is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

The current version of the App does not require an account or personal profile to use its core plant identification features.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if Luvplants' functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

If future versions introduce features such as user accounts, cloud storage, server-based image recognition, advertising, analytics, or other online services, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this Privacy Policy was most recently revised.

隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年9月3日

1. 简介

本隐私政策说明 Luvplants（以下简称“本应用”“我们”或“我们的”） 在您使用本应用时如何处理相关信息。

我们重视您的隐私。Luvplants 的植物图像识别功能采用设备端本地处理方式。

Luvplants 当前版本无需注册账号，且用于植物识别的照片不会上传至我们的服务器。

2. 相机权限

Luvplants 可能会请求访问您设备的相机。

仅当您主动选择拍摄植物照片进行识别时，本应用才会使用相机权限。

您可以随时在设备设置中拒绝或撤销相机权限。

3. 照片图库访问

Luvplants 允许您从设备的照片图库中选择已有照片，用于植物识别。

本应用只会处理您通过图片选择界面主动选择的照片。

4. 植物图像识别

通过 Luvplants 拍摄或选择的植物图片会使用设备端机器学习模型进行处理。

植物识别在您的设备本地完成。 当前版本不会将植物照片发送到我们的服务器进行识别。

5. 存储在您设备上的信息

当植物被成功识别后，Luvplants 可能会在您的设备本地保存以下信息：

  • 植物照片；
  • 植物照片的缩略图；
  • 识别出的植物名称；
  • 识别置信度；
  • 识别日期；
  • 同一种植物被发现的次数。

这些信息用于提供植物收藏、识别历史和植物卡片等应用功能。

6. 数据传输与共享

在当前版本中，Luvplants 不运营用于接收您的植物照片或本地植物识别记录的服务器。

我们不会出售您的个人信息。

我们不会将您的植物照片或识别历史用于广告或用户追踪。

我们不会主动将您本地存储的植物记录分享给广告网络、数据经纪商或第三方分析服务提供商。

7. 账号与个人信息

当前版本的 Luvplants 不要求用户创建账号。

为使用核心植物识别功能，本应用不会主动要求您提供姓名、电子邮箱地址、 电话号码或家庭住址等个人信息。

8. 广告、分析与追踪

当前版本的 Luvplants 不主动集成第三方广告系统或行为广告功能。

Luvplants 不会使用您的数据进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪。

9. 数据保留与删除

植物照片和识别记录存储在您的设备本地。

Luvplants 提供删除植物记录及相关图片的功能。

删除本应用时，本地存储的应用数据也可能被删除， 具体取决于 Apple 操作系统行为以及由用户控制的设备备份设置。

由于当前版本不维护用户账号，也不会将这些记录存储在我们的服务器上， 因此我们通常不会持有您本地植物收藏的远程副本。

10. 设备权限管理

您可以通过设备的“设置”应用管理授予 Luvplants 的权限。

如果关闭相机权限，需要拍摄新植物照片的功能可能无法使用。

11. 儿童隐私

Luvplants 并非以主动收集儿童个人信息为目的进行设计。

当前版本无需用户创建账号或个人资料即可使用核心植物识别功能。

12. 本隐私政策的变更

如果 Luvplants 的功能、数据处理方式或相关法律要求发生变化， 我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。

如果未来版本加入用户账号、云存储、服务器端图像识别、广告、 数据分析或其他在线服务，我们可能会相应更新本隐私政策。

页面顶部的“最后更新日期”表示本隐私政策最近一次修订的时间。
